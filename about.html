Who we are

MADAM is a student project about gender equality. We built it to learn, to share what we found, and to give people a place to speak without having to give their name.

The name

It reads the same both ways

Forward or backward, MADAM doesn't change. We liked that. It's how we think people should be treated, whichever side they're standing on.

The name M.A.D.A.M. (Making A Difference, Always Magnificent) represents a group that believes in the power of unity, equality, and friendship. The logo combines the female and male symbols into an infinity sign, representing that both genders are equally important and should always be treated with respect. It symbolizes the coming together of different identities, showing that differences should never be a reason for division. As M.A.D.A.M., we aim to make a difference through our teamwork, support, and shared belief that everyone deserves equal opportunities.

From our members

In our own words

Five of us made this site. Here's why it matters to each of us.

M

Maxime

A person's potential has no gender, and their dreams should have no limits.
A

Andrian

Gender equality matters to me because I believe everyone deserves to be treated equally, regardless of their gender. No one should feel less important, limited, or judged just because of who they are. I believe everyone should have the same opportunities, respect, and freedom to pursue their dreams. For me, equality is not about making one gender better than another; it is about giving everyone a fair chance. We may have differences, but those differences should never be a reason to discriminate against someone. I want to live in a society where people are respected for who they are and what they can do, not judged by their gender.
D

Dionne

Gender equality is not just about treating everyone the same, it is about making sure everyone has the same opportunities, rights, and respect regardless of gender. Even today, discrimination, stereotypes, and unequal treatment still exist, which is why we must not turn a blind eye to them. We believe that change begins when people acknowledge that the problem still exists and choose to be part of the solution. Everyone deserves to feel valued, heard, and empowered.
A

Amiel

Society is now loudly demanding for more equal treatment and wages between straight men, women, and LGBTQIA+. We must heed to these calls by advancing and empowering the rights of women and LGBTQIA+, both in school and in the workplace. When everyone is given the opportunity to participate, contribute, and thrive without prejudice, all can be more productive and harmonious; all people will be valued regardless of gender or creed.
M

Mikylla

Gender equality matters to me because no one should have to change who they are to be accepted. Gender should never decide what we can do or how much respect we get. We should all be free to be ourselves without shame. I want to help build a kinder, less judgmental world where everyone feels they belong. Without the labels, we are all different, we are all valid, and we all deserve the same respect and dignity. “Labels may well divide us in the eyes of others, but they will never in any way define our value.”
Who it's for

Everyone, honestly

This isn't women against men. Rigid gender roles hurt a lot of people: women who get paid less, men who are told not to cry, LGBTQ+ people who are told they don't belong.

If you think fairness shouldn't depend on gender, this is for you.
